# A.C.E. Recon Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-27.

This policy explains what information Ace, Private Investigations LLC ("we") handles when you use A.C.E. Recon (the "Software").

## What stays on your workstation
Case files, subject details, reports, screenshots, evidence archives and Watchtower data are stored on your
workstation. We do not receive copies of your case files or reports.

## What we receive about you
- **License and account:** your license key, plan, the hardware identifier of the workstation the key is bound to,
  the name and email given at purchase, and the version of these terms you accepted and when.
- **Usage:** the number of investigations run and their case reference IDs, app version, estimated processing cost
  and error reports you choose to send. Case reference IDs are folder names and can include the subject's surname.
- **Billing:** payments are processed by Stripe. We receive the payment status and the email and name on the
  purchase, not your card details.
- **Watchtower email alerts:** the address alerts are sent to, the monitored profile link and the alert screenshot,
  which pass through our email service to deliver the alert.

## What is sent to service providers during an investigation
To search and analyze, the Software sends parts of case data through our relay to these providers:
- web and news search: SerpApi;
- profile data collection: Apify;
- text and image analysis: OpenAI and Google.

This can include the subject's name, locations, relatives, public profile links and text, and screenshots of public
pages. Our relay forwards these requests. For billing and abuse limits it keeps the license key, workstation
identifier, case reference ID and counts of requests per case; it does not store the content of the requests. Each provider handles the data under
its own terms and data policies for business API use.

Public records searches are sent directly to the government sources (for example the Treasury OFAC list, FEC, NPPES,
SEC EDGAR, FAA and state license registries).

## How we use what we receive
To issue and verify licenses, count investigations, process payments, send Watchtower alerts, prevent abuse, provide
support and improve the Software. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention
License and billing records are kept while your license is active and as long as needed for tax, legal and dispute
purposes. Usage records are kept for 24 months.

## Security
License traffic is encrypted, service provider keys are held on our servers and not on your workstation, and access
to our records is limited to us.

## Your choices
You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention
requirements, by contacting scott@ace-pi.com. Residents of some states have additional rights; we will honor them as
the law requires.

## Information about investigation subjects
The Software processes information about investigation subjects on your behalf. You decide what is investigated and
are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so. Subject requests about case files should go to the investigator
who holds them.

## Changes
We will post changes here and the Software will ask you to accept a new version.

Contact: Ace, Private Investigations LLC, scott@ace-pi.com.
